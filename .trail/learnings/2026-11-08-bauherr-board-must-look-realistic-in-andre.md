## Decided
- Three looks were tested, and the user rejected the toy style as too playful. They chose a realistic golden hour look with hotels filling the board and the dice on the plaza.
- In the code: Bauherr TV board is in the client (bauherr-assets.ts, bauherr-camera.ts, public/bauherr/ with Poly Haven CC0 textures). Keep matte materials, real rock and snow, per-hotel landscape, and a winding road with a bridge. Avoid faceted low-poly or toy styling.
