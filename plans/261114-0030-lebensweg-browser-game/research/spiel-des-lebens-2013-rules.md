# Spiel des Lebens 2013 (German edition): rules, and what changes for Lebensweg

Source: the user's rules sheet `~/Downloads/spiel des lebens.pdf` (Hasbro 14529, © 2013, "GOL Graphic Refresh",
2 pages), read 2026-11-14. User: "we need to get closer to Spiel des Lebens". Phases 1–2 followed the 2000 US
rulebook and a fan clone of that edition; this edition differs in most rules. Board spaces and card values are not
on the sheet and not online either: `spiel-des-lebens-2013-board-and-cards.md`.

## Material
2–6 players, age 8+. 55 cards in 6 decks: Beruf, Akademischer Beruf, Trautes Heim, Luxusvilla, Kapitalanlage,
Teilen macht Freude. 25 life tiles (values hidden until everyone retired). 24 spin-to-win marks and a strip.
6 cars, pink and blue pegs, board with 3 mountains, a bridge and 7 buildings, a wheel 1–10. Loan slips (20,000),
raise slips (10,000), money.

## Setup
- Each player: a car and a peg, **10,000**, **2 Teilen macht Freude cards** (kept secret).
- Life tiles shuffled face down; **3 tiles face down beside the Millionärsvilla**.
- Highest spin starts; play goes clockwise.

## A turn
1. Optional, at the start: **buy a Kapitalanlage** for 10,000 (pick a number card; one per player). Whenever anyone
   spins that number, its owner gets **5,000**. Retired players keep it and still spin for it on their turn.
2. Spin and move; at forks follow the arrows.
3. Do the field (by colour, below).
- Loans any time on your turn: 20,000 each, repaid with 5,000 interest (25,000), any time or at retirement.
- **Police**: whoever spins a 10 pays **5,000** to the player holding the Polizistin card, if she claims it in time.

## Start (first turn)
- **START: BERUF**: draw one Beruf card at random (job, salary, income tax), spin, move.
- **START: UNIVERSITÄT**: the bank pays the tuition with **100,000 in loans**; spin, move. At the STOP
  "Akademischen Beruf wählen": draw 2 Akademischer Beruf cards, keep one.

## Fields
| Colour | Field | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| green | Zahltag | Salary (plus 10,000 per raise slip), when passing or landing. |
| green | Zahltag – Gehaltserhöhung | Also a raise slip (+10,000 per payday), unless the card's top salary is reached. |
| yellow | Du erhältst / Zahle | Money from / to the bank, amount on the field (e.g. Aktiengewinne +100,000; Ferienhaus −120,000). |
| yellow | Leben | Take a life tile (e.g. "Doktorarbeit!"). Pile empty: take one from another player. |
| yellow | Einkommensteuer | Pay the tax printed on your career card. |
| yellow | Steuerrückzahlung | The bank pays you that tax amount. |
| yellow | Gefeuert! | Raise slips back; draw a new Beruf card at random (no academic careers). |
| yellow | Baby (son, daughter, twins, adoption) | Pegs into the car, one life tile, and **every other player gives 5,000** (twins too: one tile, 5,000 each). |
| yellow | Superdreh | Everyone may bet: lander first, then clockwise, one mark on a number (cards allow 2 or 4), stake up to 50,000 to the bank; the lander spins; a hit pays **10×** the stake. |
| orange | STOP fields | Must stop even with steps left; do the field; spin again and move on. |
| blue | Klage | Sue a player of your choice for **100,000**; they pay unless they play a Befreit card. |

## STOPs and forks (orange)
- **Akademischen Beruf wählen** (end of the university path): 2 cards, keep one.
- **Du heiratest**: a life tile and the partner's peg; spin for gifts: **8–10 every player gives 10,000, 5–7 gives
  5,000, 1–4 nothing** (givers borrow if short).
- **Trautes Heim kaufen**: look at the free starter-home cards, pick one, pay (borrow if short).
- **Noch mal studieren**: choose to study again (pay **50,000**, take the study path) or go on. At the path's end,
  **Wähle einen neuen Beruf**: an academic career (draw 2, keep one, or keep the old job) or a **raise of 20,000**
  (2 raise slips).
- **Familienweg**: the children path or the main road.
- **Luxusvilla kaufen**: optionally sell the starter home at its sale value and buy a luxury home. Never two houses.
- **Riskanter Weg**: the risky path with many Superdreh fields, or the safe path with more family time.

## Teilen macht Freude cards (one use each, secret, max one card against a player per turn; ties: spin, highest plays)
- **Du erhältst**: when another player lands on a yellow Du erhältst field, they give you half of it.
- **Zahle**: when you land on a yellow Zahle field, play it on another player: they pay half to the bank.
- **Befreit**: cancels a Du erhältst or Zahle card played on you; some also protect against a Klage.
- **Superdreh**: place 2 or 4 marks on a Superdreh bet.

## Retirement and winning
At the road's end: career cards and raise slips out (Teilen macht Freude and Kapitalanlage cards stay), repay all
loans with interest, **sell the house to the bank at the price on its card**, **10,000 per child**, then choose:
- **Millionärsvilla**: the first player in looks at the 3 tiles there and keeps one, the second keeps one of two, the
  third gets the last, later ones nothing. Tiles here are not safe: players still on the road with an empty pile take
  from them.
- **Altersheim**: no extra tile, but nobody can take yours.
When everyone has retired: cash plus life tile values; the richest wins.

## Lebensweg today (2000 edition) against 2013
| Topic | Lebensweg now | 2013 German edition | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Careers | career deck with perks + separate salary deck, pick 1 of 3 | Beruf drawn at random, or Akademischer Beruf 1 of 2; one card = job, salary, tax | rewrite cards, perks go (Polizistin stays) |
| Start | Studium (tuition 40,000) / Ausbildung | Universität (100,000 in loans) / Beruf | rules + numbers |
| Raises | none (Bühne's streak) | Gehaltserhöhung paydays, 20,000 raise at Wähle einen neuen Beruf | new |
| Taxes | tax field, to Buchhaltung | Einkommensteuer and Steuerrückzahlung | change |
| Houses | random house at Hauskauf, home insurance | pick a Trautes Heim; optional Luxusvilla; sold at retirement | rewrite |
| Insurance | car and home insurance | none | remove |
| Stocks | stock deck 1–9, boom/crash fields | Kapitalanlage 10,000, 5,000 per spin of its number | rewrite |
| Spin to win | none | Superdreh fields, bets, 10× | new |
| Share cards | none | Teilen macht Freude, 2 per player | new (phone hand) |
| Lawsuit | none | Klage 100,000, Befreit protects | new |
| Wedding | partner + life token | + gift spin from every player | change |
| Babies | child(ren) + token | + 5,000 from every player | change |
| Study again | night school field | Noch mal studieren STOP and path | rewrite |
| Life tokens | 25 (+7), values open | 25 tiles, values hidden until the end | change |
| Retirement | Villa (estate to richest) / Landhaus (+1) | house sold, 10,000 per child, Millionärsvilla (3 tiles) / Altersheim (safe) | rewrite |
| Spinner gap | IT-Beratung collects 50,000 | not in this edition | remove |
| Board | 2000 board, 147 spaces × 1.3 | 2013 board (space list pending) | redo board, stretch, balance |
