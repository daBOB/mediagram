# Original rules and numbers: Hasbro/MB "Life, the Game of" (edition 2000)

Date: 2026-11-14. Scope: US Hasbro/MB Life. German "Spiel des Lebens" not covered (no German rulebook found).

## 0. Edition verdict

**Board matches the 2000 printing** ("Life, the Game of", © 2000 Hasbro, rev 09/00 Rev.1, code 04000-I) [S1]. Its numbers and rules text are the 1991 ruleset [S2][S3] with two changes: a $20,000 fee printed on Night School, and the red space renamed CAREER CHOICE (1991: JOB SEARCH). Example card changed from Superstar (1991) to Entertainer (2000), both $70,000.

Fit to your board, by layer:
| Board layer | In 1991/2000 text? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| College $40K vs career, LIFE tiles, 4 Millionaire tiles, Millionaire Estates / Countryside Acres | Yes [S1][S2] | matches |
| Red trio CAREER CHOICE → GET MARRIED → BUY A HOUSE | Yes, exactly 3 red spaces [S1] | matches your order |
| Night School (pay $20K) | Yes, 2000 only [S1] | matches 2000 |
| Babies (boy/girl +1, twins +2, LIFE tile each) | Yes [S1] | matches |
| Family path vs Adventure path; Risky road vs Safe route (forks) | **No** in 1991 or 2000 text | not in either rulebook. Family/Risky STOPs exist in the 2017 Classic guide [S5]. Treat as fan-clone or own design, not 2000 |
| Ausbildung 3 fields / Studium 10 fields | Not in text (no path fields given) | unverifiable from rulebook; layout only |

Excluded editions: 1977 (Share the Wealth, Revenge, Millionaire Tycoon, number strip, Bankrupt) [S4]; 2017 Classic (Spin to Win, pets, 200K start, Millionaire Mansion, retirement bonuses 400K–100K, babies by spin, wedding cash gifts) [S5]. Groupgames101/gamerules numbers ($200K start, $100K tuition, $1M Millionaire) are from later printings; do not use [S7].
