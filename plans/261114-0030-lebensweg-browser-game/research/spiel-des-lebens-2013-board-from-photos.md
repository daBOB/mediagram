## The numbers on the board are slot numbers, not space numbers
The spaces carry **no numbers**. The numbers 1–12 are printed only where the 3D pieces go: **1–7** are the building plots,
**8–11** the three mountains and the bridge, and **12** is the lake, where the wheel's mountain stands. "Field 12" is the
wheel's slot. Read on B and A.

## Path and forks (short)
- **Two starts** meet at a ZAHLTAG. Universität: 9 spaces, then STOP *Hochschul-Beruf wählen*. Beruf: 3 spaces.
- The main road runs up the right edge (racetrack, bridge, wedding), across the top (mountains), and down the left edge.
  It then winds through the centre around the lake, crosses the left middle (risky/safe fork) and ends bottom left at
  RENTE.
- **4 forks**, each closing again:
  1. **STOP Noch mal studieren** (#39): the study lane N1–N7 (ends at STOP *Wähle einen neuen Beruf*) or the outer lane
     O1–O7. Both lanes are 7 spaces. They meet at KLAGE #40.
  2. **STOP Familienweg** (#63): the family lane F1–F6 or the other lane W1–W4. They meet at Superdreh #64.
  3. **STOP Riskanter Weg ODER sicherer Weg** (#82): the risky lane R1–R8 (4 Superdreh) or the safe lane S1–S4. They meet
     at Neuer Swimmingpool #83.
  4. The two starts (U / B lanes) meet at ZAHLTAG #1.
- STOPs without a fork: Hochschul-Beruf wählen (U10), Du heiratest (#11), Trautes Heim kaufen (#19), Kaufe ein neues
  Haus (#72).
