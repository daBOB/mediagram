# Spiel des Lebens 2013 (Hasbro 14529): card values from photos

Date: 2026-11-14. Goal: transcribe the card values from photos found online.
Result: **only the Lebenskärtchen could be read**, and only from a 2015 printing of 14529. No card face of
Beruf, Akademischer Beruf, Trautes Heim, Luxusvilla, Kapitalanlage or Teilen macht Freude was found in any
reachable photo. Nothing below is guessed. Every gap is marked missing.

Evidence folder (E): `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-andre-Workspace-spieltisch/48628d26-fcfb-47ad-b120-1d9a5e871a2c/scratchpad/sdl/evidence-cards/`

## Which printing is which (read this first)

Product 14529 exists in **two printings with different card decks**. Values may differ between them.

| Printing | Box | Deck names on card backs | Card design | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2013 (the user's rules sheet)** | "Das Original!" rosette, cartoon family in red car, signpost **Beruf / Universität**; later copies have a red sticker "Die Kinder haben abgestimmt! Jetzt mit den Lieblingsberufen unserer Kids!" | Beruf, Akademischer Beruf, Trautes Heim, **Luxusvilla**, Kapitalanlage, Teilen macht Freude (blog text) | white card, logo on top, coloured band with deck name (green band "TEILEN MACHT FREUDE", orange band "LUXUSVILLA") | E/blog-spiele-und-spieler-2015-Einsatz.jpg, E/kleinanzeigen-3567764390-2013-box-kinder-haben-abgestimmt-luxusvilla-card-back.jpg |
| **2015 (seller: "2015 Hasbro 14529")** | "Seit 1960 Das Original" badge, rendered 3D-style family, signposts **Sicherheit / Risiko** | Beruf, **Hochschul-Beruf**, Trautes Heim, **Haus**, Kapital-Anlage, Teilen macht Freude | full-colour gradient card with an icon (green, purple, yellow, blue) | E/kleinanzeigen-3534034157-sicherheit-risiko-box-deck-backs-haus-hochschul-beruf.jpg, E/youtube-bDtorNb3UE0-t120-sicherheit-risiko-deck-backs-trautes-heim-haus-hochschul-beruf.jpg |

The 2015 printing keeps the rules shape: Superdreh marks, Kapitalanlage, Teilen macht Freude, two house decks.
But it renames two decks, so it may also change values. **Treat 2015 values as "same product number, other
printing"**, not as confirmed 2013 values.

Excluded, not used for any value:
- "Dein Leben, dein Weg" / "Wähle deinen eigenen Lebensweg" (Hasbro F0800, Aktions-, Berufs- and Hauskarten, ©2021):
  a newer game. Seen in Kleinanzeigen 3566303531 and 3564237416.
- The edition with pets (2–4 players) and the electronic banking edition (400,000 start): different games.
- MB editions from 1978 and the 1990s (Versicherungen, Statussymbol, Schikane cards): different games.
- The Steam/digital "Game of Life" career numbers that turned up in a search: not a photo, and not this edition.
