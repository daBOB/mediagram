# Spiel des Lebens 2013: board and cards (online search)

Searched 2026-11-14 by a research agent; checked against the rules sheet. Result: **the board order, the space count
and the card values are not online** in any source it could reach. They need photos of the board and cards.

## Found
- The official sheet is online too (same file as the user's PDF): Hasbro 14529i398, dated 19/08/2013.
  https://www.hasbro.com/common/documents/dad261621c4311ddbd0b0800200c9a66/651AB4B150569047F54C233888601443.pdf
- One career card is pictured on the sheet: **Autoverkäufer, salary 20,000** (tax unreadable).
- Deck sizes, secondary only: a 2014 review says 22 Teilen macht Freude, 9 Kapitalanlage and 6 each of Beruf,
  Akademischer Beruf, Trautes Heim and Luxusvilla (22 + 9 + 4 × 6 = 55, which matches the sheet's 55 cards).
  http://spiele-und-spieler.blogspot.com/2015/02/das-spiel-des-lebens-2014.html
- Teilen macht Freude has "five kinds"; the sheet names Du erhältst, Zahle, Befreit and Superdreh (2 or 4 marks).
  The fifth is likely Superdreh ×2 and ×4 counted apart; not confirmed.
- Field types, STOPs, forks and the amounts the sheet gives: `spiel-des-lebens-2013-rules.md` (all primary).

## Not usable
- English "The Game of Life" rules of 2010 (54 cards, Share the Wealth, Starter Homes): a sibling edition, values
  not the same as the German 2013 deck.
  https://www.hasbro.com/common/documents/dad261421c4311ddbd0b0800200c9a66/421541C319B9F36910397125043F45A3.pdf
- German electronic bank-card edition (400,000 start, 200,000 tuition): a different game.
- BoardGameGeek and libble.de refused automated reads (403). No GitHub reimplementation of this edition found.

## Corrections to the agent's notes (checked against the sheet)
- Trautes Heim kaufen: look at **all free** starter-home cards and pick one (the agent wrote "draw 2").
- Luxusvilla: the starter home is sold at the **sale value** on its card; at retirement a house is sold at the
  **purchase price** on its card (sheet, Rente step 4).
